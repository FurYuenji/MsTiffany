# MsTiffany

轻量工具，从TXT文件随机抽取姓名和英语单词，适用于课堂点名、单词抽查等场景。  
A lightweight tool for randomly extracting names and English words from TXT files, suitable for classroom roll calls, word spot checks, etc.

## 预览图 | Preview
![MsTiffany预览](https://s3.bmp.ovh/imgs/2025/12/13/3f6923f3294d553c.jpg)

## ✨ 特点 | Features
- **内置高中外研版全量单词**：涵盖高中各单元外研版英语单词  
  **Included Full Set of Senior High Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press Words**：Covers all units of senior high English words from Foreign Language Teaching and Research Press  
- 随机抽取，自动去重；离线运行，操作简单；兼容新旧浏览器及教室设备  
  Random extraction with automatic deduplication; offline operation; easy to use; compatible with new/old browsers and classroom devices
- **采用姓名编码混淆技术，防止对信息的简单篡改**
  **Name encoding obfuscation is adopted to prevent simple tampering with information**
